# Private objective evaluation

`measurePlayerObjectives(state, playerId, additionalEvidence?)` reads canonical current positions and actual settled observations. `evaluateObjective(measurements, objectiveId)` returns exact numerical checks and an eligibility status. `evaluateAllObjectives` evaluates the same private measurements for all eight objectives. The functions never change balances, history, counters, RNG, revisions or match results.

The recipient projection includes only its selected objective evaluation in `self.financialDetails.objectiveEvaluation`. Rules own readable labels for every check; the engine supplies each label, status and pass/fail flag. The browser renders qualitative “Met”/“Still needed” progress alongside the exact requirements without comparing financial values or awarding victory. Before selection, or when safe aggregate arithmetic overflows, that field is null. It never appears in public player summaries. The engine supports explicit Independent and Executive Owner career finales. It never projects another player's requirements. Ordinary eligibility and ordinary economic actions never award victory.

## Sources and integration

| Input | Authoritative owner | Measurement |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Cash, public funds, business/property ownership and company loans | Current `GameState.finance` | Current face-value liquid capital; attributed assets and liabilities; company equity; control; performing personal-loan principal |
| Employment, work, living costs, investment receipts and company operating profit | Actual `progress.byPlayer[*].quarters` | Last four completed quarters; missing quarters contribute zero, never an annualized projection. Company operating history is deduplicated across its actual owners. |
| Asset-borrower property principal, cash reserves and arrears | `collectPlayerObjectiveEvidence` reads current owned, unsold property records and asset-bank loans | `ObjectivePropertyClaims`, aggregated once per asset. Legacy personal mortgages remain personal liabilities and reduce their related property equity once. Current property accounting has no separate operating-payable record. |
| Genuine player lending exposure and interest received | Canonical `PlayerLoan.fundedTransactionId` and `settledInterestReceipts` | Actual confirmed cash-funded transaction, original supplied capital, current principal and arrears, beneficial borrower, and per-loan paid interest. Collection of old interest contributes lifetime received profit but not fresh recurring income. |
| Sales, basis, fees, exposure and reference quotes | Canonical `PlayerProgress.disposalEvidence` from public-market, property and business closes | A complete signed ledger, projected at the evaluated revision. It preserves transaction and slice identity, gross price, fees, actual acquisition cost, full settled exposure and independent player-sale reference values. |

`ObjectiveEvidence` is an internal engine contract, **not a command or browser payload**. Measurement automatically collects canonical property and funded player-loan evidence. Supplements cannot replace a current property or any registered loan; only future unknown-ID NPC notes retain a supplemental integration seam. New matches reject supplemental disposal ledgers; numerical fixtures explicitly omit the canonical ledger. No endpoint accepts supplemental evidence.

Loan origination initializes an empty receipt history. Actual payment allocation appends the quarter, total interest paid and fresh current interest paid; old unpaid interest is collected first. Snapshot invariants require history for transaction-tagged player loans and validate safe money, unique chronological quarters and current interest no larger than received interest. Old missing history is never reconstructed. Normal snapshot serialization retains these records with their loan.

Quarterly source observations separately mark personal mortgage service already deducted from property income. Asset-bank payoff interest has no personal marker, so refinancing cannot reduce the evaluator's deduction for unrelated required personal debt service.

Public-market exposure slices track dates and values alongside the existing fungible portfolio and weighted aggregate cost basis. They never price trades or change account balances. Actual settlement revalues those slices to the unchanged aggregate mark. Opening exposure can first qualify after Q1; a mid-quarter Qn acquisition first qualifies after settling Qn+1. A sale before settlement cannot borrow age from an opening balance or an older acquisition. Missing or inconsistent old fund evidence restarts observed age conservatively rather than inventing history.

A close groups its acquisition slices under one transaction identity. The evaluator nets all signed slice gains before testing profitable-close count, and applies the $50,000 gross threshold to qualified exposure aggregated across that close. Player-sale gross and gain credit are bounded by the independent current stake quote. Property gross is its sale price; released asset cash and payoff interest remain separate gain inputs, so reserves do not inflate material sale size. Genuine non-player bargains retain their actual recorded acquisition cost.

Negotiated player purchases and primary ownership grants also record an independent entry-value floor separately from actual paid acquisition cost. Positive objective gain uses the greater of paid cost and that floor, including on a later NPC exit. Dated slices carry and proportionally allocate the floor through partial sales, dilution, ownership returns and personally funded expansion. This does not change cash, company capital, actual realized gains or negative disposal losses; genuine NPC acquisitions retain their actual bargain cost.

Business acquisition slices retain prior-owner transfer references. An observed return records only the transferred fraction along that ownership cycle. Projection splits affected earlier dispositions into genuine and circular exposure without changing cash, cost or historical loss. Positive circular credit is excluded; actual losses remain. This tracks observed transfers, not inferred player intent. Future joint-property transfers can use the same canonical records and ownership-transfer helper.

Existing raw `realizedGainsCents`, `financingInterestReceivedCents` and `profitableDisposalAssetIds` do not independently establish qualified progress. Missing funding, paid-interest, seasoning or reference evidence contributes no positive qualified progress; evidence gaps remain explicit. A stale ledger, duplicate loan/transaction evidence, inconsistent company history or malformed money rejects measurement. Callers must handle that failure without granting eligibility or exposing private details.

## Approved interpretations

The numerical targets remain owned by `rules/completion.ts`; the newly approved section 18.5 deal-count definitions live in `rules/objectiveQualifications.ts`. The approved clarifications are recorded in canonical documentation commit `478cdc4`, to be integrated by the parent task.

| Requirement | Interpretation |
| --- | --- |
| Recurring sources | Count actual receipts from currently retained economic positions. Fully disposed funds/assets, repaid loans and departed labor cease recurring contribution; no projected future return is substituted. |
| Living-cost protection | Greater of current annual living costs and actual living costs owed in the trailing four completed quarters. Missing quarters contribute zero. |
| Independent | Actual non-employment cash flow after required personal debt service must cover the protected-cost multiple; property mortgage service already deducted from attributed property income is not deducted twice. Require no personal overdue obligations and rely only on performing sources. |
| Founder | Control, attributed equity, actual profit, leverage, positive consecutive quarters and performing status must hold for one company. Profits from different companies cannot be combined to satisfy it. |
| Relevant borrower | Personal overdue obligations gate eligibility. Company arrears gate the qualifying Founder company; property arrears gate the Magnate portfolio. An unrelated passive asset default does not globally veto every other objective. Its distributions are excluded from performing recurring sources. |
| Debt and claims | Deduct personal and attributed asset principal, unpaid interest and operating/work payables once. Omit unpaid labor receivables from objective assets until collected; the canonical payable still exists. Overdue lender claims have zero objective asset value while remaining collectible in canonical state. |
| Lending diversity | Group holdings and recurring income by beneficial borrower. Count a distinct performing funded deal only after at least $50,000 was originally supplied by the player; group its split tranches. Preserve lifetime actually received qualified interest after repayment. |
| Disposal progress | Positive credit requires an actual settled quarter of exposure, genuine economic substance and an independent stake quote for player transfers. Net losses and transaction fees reduce progress. Count a profitable disposal only at $50,000 or more gross proceeds. Genuine non-player bargain cost remains its actual cost. |
| Concentration | Use exact Decimal cross-multiplication. Capital Allocator compares invested holdings with invested equity. Wealth Architect includes cash as a holding against net worth; cash supplies no productive category. Material positive holdings establish productive categories. |

## Explicit career finales

The shared `PURSUE_FINALE` payload wraps a typed `RESIGN_LABOR`, `ACCEPT_CAREER_OFFER` or nested-only `CONFIRM_INDEPENDENCE` action. It accepts no objective, measurement, balance or outcome. The engine derives the actor's selected objective and rejects unsupported or extra payload fields. Ordinary resignation and career acceptance keep their existing effects and spotlight behavior; already-jobless players can pass without a separate ordinary confirmation action.

| Route | Actual owning effect | Required resulting position |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Independent resignation | Existing labor resignation, including equal-cost external management and retained worker claims | Canonical eligibility before and after; no employment/owner work or employment pay after the action |
| Independent confirmation | No financial change for an already-not-working actor | Same canonical income, debt, diversification and reserve checks |
| Executive Owner | Existing career offer acceptance; actual $100,000 secondary consideration and separate purchase fee | Executive role linked to the new genuinely funded ownership asset, required actual earned salary/non-salary history, ownership and post-cost liquidity |

`game/finalization.ts` owns the internal `EconomicActionFinalization<Facts>` / `EconomicActionFinalizer<Facts>` contract. The owning transition supplies original state, unadvanced draft, actor, economic journals and realized typed facts. `applyCareerCommand` calls an optional finalizer immediately before `advanceSpotlight` and returns its result verbatim. A finalizer must include the economic journals on success or return the original state with empty journals on rejection.

`finalePrecondition` validates own spotlight and selected-objective eligibility. Owning transitions retain their ordinary closing, active-transaction and consent guards. `finalizeObjectiveAction(input, route)` independently checks preconditions, requires an unadvanced draft, verifies trusted route facts, and measures canonical post-effect eligibility. Explicit failure rejects the entire action. Success sets the public winner/quarter result, clears the round and increments the original revision exactly once, before any spotlight advancement or quarter settlement, including Q32. No success/failure fallback creates an ordinary close. Sealed auctions remain ordinary.

The generic route interface retains ordinary objective requirements after closing; Founder's approved full-exit alternative needs its separate post-exit rule when integrated. The coordinator adds no network evidence input, future-income credit, result-specific cash calculation or automatic settlement. The server accepts the strictly nested wrapper through the existing authenticated command queue, canonical hashing and SQLite receipt path. Both wrapper and economic action reject unknown fields; confirmation remains nested-only. UI finale controls and the separate Q32 draw/FINISHED operational persistence branch are not included in this slice.
